**Affirmative (ইতিবাচক/হ্যাঁ বোধক)** হলো সেই ধরনের বাক্য যা দিয়ে **কোনো সত্য বা কাজকে হ্যাঁ বোঝানো হয়** – অর্থাৎ, "না" বা নেতিবাচক কিছু থাকে না।

 **ইংরেজিতে সংজ্ঞা:**  
An *affirmative sentence* states that something is **true or positive**. It does **not** contain a negative word like *not, no, never*.

 **উদাহরণ (বাংলা + ইংরেজি):**  
 ```
 আমি ভাত খাই। → I eat rice. 
 সে স্কুল যায়। → He goes to school.
```

**Note:**  
Affirmative বাক্যে কোনো **not** বা নেতিবাচক শব্দ থাকবে না – এটাই মূল চিহ্ন।


### NonStop Practice 

### 01. He does not play football. ```→ He plays football.```
### 02. She never tells a lie. ```→ She always tells the truth.```
### 03. Nobody dislikes him. ```→ Everybody likes him.```
### 04. I have nothing to say. ```→ I have something to say.```
### 05. He is not honest. ```→ He is dishonest.```
### 06. There is no boy without a pen. ```→ Every boy has a pen.```
### 07. None but a fool can do this. ```→ Only a fool can do this.```
### 08. He did not fail to help me. ```→ He helped me.```
### 09. I cannot but laugh. ```→ I laugh.```
### 10. You cannot help smiling. ```→ You smile.```
