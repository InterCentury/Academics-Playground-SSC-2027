## c) Population Problems in Bangladesh

**Bangladesh** is a small country with a very large population. It is one of the most densely populated countries in the world. The main problems caused by overpopulation are **unemployment**, **poverty**, **food shortage**, and **lack of housing**. Many people do not have jobs because there are not enough opportunities for everyone. The demand for food is high, but the supply is limited. Cities are overcrowded, and slums are growing everywhere. Our natural resources like land, water, and forests are under pressure. The government provides **education** and **healthcare**, but it is difficult to reach everyone. The **illiteracy rate** is also high, and many people are unaware of family planning. To solve this problem, we need to control the **birth rate** through family planning programs and educate people about the dangers of overpopulation. Everyone should understand that a small family is a happy family. Population growth must be managed for a better future.

## e) Causes of Road Accidents in Bangladesh

**Road accidents** are a common and tragic problem in Bangladesh. Every day, many people lose their lives in road accidents. The main causes of road accidents are **reckless driving**, **over-speeding**, and **violation of traffic rules**. Many drivers do not follow traffic signals and drive dangerously. **Overloading** passengers and goods in vehicles is another major cause. **Bad roads**, potholes, and poor lighting also contribute to accidents. The lack of proper **road signs** confuses drivers and pedestrians. Many vehicles are old and not roadworthy, making them unsafe. Drivers are often tired or distracted, leading to loss of control. **Lack of awareness** among pedestrians also causes accidents. To solve this problem, the government should enforce traffic laws strictly, improve road conditions, and ensure regular vehicle inspections. Public awareness campaigns about road safety are also necessary. Everyone should follow traffic rules to save lives. **Road safety is everyone's responsibility**.

## i) Floods in Bangladesh

**Floods** are a common natural disaster in Bangladesh. Every year, many parts of the country suffer from severe floods. The main cause of floods is **heavy rainfall** during the monsoon season. Melting snow from the Himalayas and the overflow of rivers like the **Padma, Jamuna, and Meghna** also cause floods. Deforestation and siltation of riverbeds have worsened the problem. Floods cause massive damage to life and property. Houses are destroyed, crops are washed away, and roads become unusable. Many people lose their homes and belongings. Farm animals die, and people suffer from **hunger** and **diseases** like diarrhea and cholera. Communication and transport systems break down, making rescue work difficult. The government and NGOs provide relief by distributing food, water, and medicine. To prevent floods, we need to plant more trees, build dams, and dredge rivers. Despite the destruction, floods also make the soil fertile for agriculture. Floods are both a curse and a blessing for our country. We must find permanent solutions to reduce flood damage.
