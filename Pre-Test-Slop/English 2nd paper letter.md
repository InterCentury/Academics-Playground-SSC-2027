### a) Letter Thanking Your Friend for the Birthday Gift

```
Dear Rahim,

I hope this letter finds you in good health and high spirits. I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for the wonderful birthday gift you sent me.

It was a pleasant surprise to receive the beautiful watch from you on my birthday. I was overjoyed when I opened the parcel. The watch is not only stylish but also very useful. I have been wearing it every day since I received it. Every time I look at it, I am reminded of our beautiful friendship and your love for me.

My birthday celebration was made even more special by your thoughtful gift. I cannot thank you enough for remembering my birthday and sending such a lovely present from so far away. Your gesture touched my heart deeply.

I hope we can meet soon and spend some quality time together. Please convey my best regards to your parents and love to your younger brother.

Thank you once again for your wonderful gift and your precious friendship.

Your loving friend,
Kamal
```

### b) Letter Describing the Picnic/Study Tour You Have Enjoyed

```
Dear Hasan,

I hope you are well. I am writing to share with you the wonderful experience of our recent study tour to Cox's Bazar.

Our school arranged a study tour for the students of Class X on 15th January. We started our journey early in the morning from Dhaka by a comfortable bus. There were forty students and three teachers. We reached Cox's Bazar in the afternoon. The scenery on the way was simply breathtaking. We saw green fields, rivers, and small hills.

The next morning, we went to the beach. The vast blue sea, the endless waves, and the golden sand made our hearts full of joy. We walked along the beach, collected shells, and took many photographs. Some of us even swam in the sea. We also visited the Buddhist temple and the local fish market. The journey was both educational and enjoyable. We had delicious food at a local restaurant.

It was an unforgettable experience. I wish you were with us. I hope we can plan another trip together soon.

Your friend,
Shuvo
```

### d) Letter About Importance of Learning English

```
Dear Sumon,

I hope you are fine. I am writing to discuss an important topic—the importance of learning English.

English is an international language. It is the most widely spoken language in the world today. Without knowledge of English, we cannot communicate with people from other countries. It is the language of science, technology, business, and education.

In Bangladesh, learning English is very important for our education and career. All the good books on science, medicine, and technology are written in English. To get a good job in any reputed organization, we need to know English. It also helps us to use the internet and access information from around the world. Moreover, English is essential for higher studies abroad.

I think we should practice English every day by reading books, newspapers, and watching English programs. We should also try to speak in English with our friends and teachers.

I hope you will also take English seriously and improve your skills.

Your friend,
Jahid
```

### f) Letter Describing Prize Giving Ceremony

```
Dear Tarek,

I hope you are in good health. I am writing to tell you about the prize giving ceremony held at our school last Friday.

The ceremony was organized in our school auditorium. The function began at 10:00 am with the recitation from the Holy Quran. The Headmaster welcomed the guests and gave a brief speech about the achievements of the students. Our chief guest was the District Education Officer.

The most exciting part was the prize distribution. Students who had achieved good results in academics, sports, and cultural activities were awarded prizes. I was very happy because I received a prize for securing the first position in the annual examination. The chief guest handed me a trophy and a certificate. I felt very proud and motivated.

There were also cultural performances by the students. Some students sang songs, recited poems, and performed dramas. The ceremony ended with a vote of thanks.

It was a memorable day for me. I wish you were there to share my joy.

Your friend,
Mamun
```
