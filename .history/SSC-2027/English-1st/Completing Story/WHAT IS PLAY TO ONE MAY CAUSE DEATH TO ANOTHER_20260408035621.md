### **WHAT IS PLAY TO ONE MAY CAUSE DEATH TO ANOTHER**

There was a pond full of frogs. Some of them often put their heads out of the water and croaked loudly. One day, some boys were playing by the side of the pond. When they heard the frogs croaking, they began to throw stones at them. All the frogs at once went down into the water. But the boys remained standing there with stones in their hands. The new game was a great fun to them. They went on pelting the frogs with stones when they raised their head above the water. Thus, many frogs were hit hard and a few were killed. At last, an old frog raised its head and said, "Oh boys please! Please! Stop the cruel fun. Don't throw stones at us." "It is a play. We are enjoying it," said the boys. "What is play to you causes death to us. This is why, you should stop it," answered the frog. Being ashamed, they left the cruel game and went away.






### (Simplified) **What is Play to One May Cause Death to Another**

There was a pond full of many frogs. Sometimes, the frogs would put their heads out of the water and make loud noises. One day, some boys were playing near the pond. When they heard the frogs, they started throwing stones at them for fun. 

The frogs were very scared and hid under the water. But the boys did not stop. They waited for the frogs to come up for air and then threw more stones. Many frogs were hurt, and some even died.

Finally, an old frog looked out and said, "Please stop! This is very cruel. Do not throw stones at us." The boys replied, "But we are just playing. We are having fun!" The old frog answered, "Your fun is killing us. Please think about that." The boys felt very bad and ashamed. They stopped the game and walked away.

**Moral of the story:**
-> What is fun for you might be harmful to others.
